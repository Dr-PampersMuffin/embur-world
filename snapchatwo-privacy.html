Liveness Verification

Snapchatwo uses a proprietary real-time liveness detection system to confirm that every verified user is a living, breathing human being — not a bot, AI avatar, deepfake, or impersonator.

What Liveness Verification Does

  • Confirms you are physically present using your device camera
  • Detects real facial movement and depth — not a static photo or video replay
  • Issues a time-limited verification token upon successful completion
  • Marks your profile with a verified badge visible to other users

What We Do NOT Do

Liveness verification is not facial recognition and is not biometric data collection. We do not store facial geometry, facial templates, biometric identifiers, or any data that can be used to identify you by your face outside of Snapchatwo. The liveness check confirms your presence — it does not record or retain your facial data. All liveness processing occurs in real time and the raw frame data is discarded immediately after verification.

Face ID / Touch ID Login

If you enable biometric login (Face ID or Touch ID), your authentication credentials are stored exclusively in the iOS Keychain on your device using Apple's Secure Enclave. Snapchatwo never receives, transmits, or stores your biometric data. Face ID authentication is processed entirely by Apple's operating system — we only receive a success or failure signal.

Camera Access

Camera access is the foundation of Snapchatwo's authenticity guarantee.

Why We Require Camera Access

  • Snap capture: All content on Snapchatwo must be captured live through the in-app camera
  • Liveness verification: Real-time presence confirmation during account setup
  • Snap Code scanning: QR code scanning to add friends
  • Profile photo: Profile pictures must be live-captured

What We Do NOT Use the Camera For

The camera is never activated in the background. It is never accessed when the app is not open and in active use. We never use the camera for surveillance, advertising profiling, or any purpose other than the features listed above. Camera access can be revoked at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy → Camera → Snapchatwo.

Microphone Access

Microphone access is requested only when recording video snaps. Audio is captured solely as part of video content you actively choose to record and post. No audio is captured passively or in the background.

Messages & Communications

Direct Messages

Snapchatwo supports direct messaging between users. Message content is stored on our servers to enable message delivery and conversation history. We do not sell, analyze for advertising, or share message content with third parties. Messages between users are private and not visible to other users.

Group Chats

Group chat messages are stored and visible to all members of the group. When you leave a group, you no longer receive new messages but your prior messages may remain visible to remaining members until the group is deleted.

Snap Replies

When you send a snap reply to another user's content, the reply is delivered as a direct message and stored accordingly.

Safety note: While we do not monitor message content in real time, we reserve the right to review messages reported by users as abusive, illegal, or in violation of our Terms of Service. We cooperate with law enforcement requests as required by applicable law.

Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information:

  • All data transmitted between the app and our servers is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher
  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt — never stored in plain text
  • Authentication tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain via Expo SecureStore
  • Media files are stored via Cloudinary with access controls
  • Our backend infrastructure is hosted on Railway with restricted access
  • Liveness tokens are single-use and time-limited
  • Regular security reviews of our codebase and infrastructure

No system is 100% secure. In the event of a data breach that affects your personal information, we will notify you as required by applicable law, typically within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach.
