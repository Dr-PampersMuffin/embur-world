Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our baseline, not an afterthought.

Last updated: 30 October 2026
👋

1. Introduction

Dealzaty is an independent deals aggregator that surfaces publicly visible price-drop information from stores operating in Kuwait. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or partnered with any store listed in the app.

Dealzaty is operated by Embur. This Privacy Policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Dealzaty you agree to the practices described here.

📊

2. Information We Collect

We collect only what is needed to run the app.

  • Device push token issued by Apple Push Notification service, used only to send the price-drop alerts you ask for
  • Your watches, the items or categories you choose to follow, stored on our servers and linked to your push token so we can alert you
  • Language preference and cart items, stored only on your device
  • Anonymous diagnostics, basic crash and performance data that does not identify you personally

Dealzaty requires no account or registration. We do not collect your name, phone number, or payment information.

🚫

3. What We Never Do

  • Require an account or login
  • Request or use your device location
  • Use an advertising identifier (IDFA) or serve targeted ads
  • Track you across other apps or websites
  • Sell, rent, or trade your information, ever
⚙️

4. How We Use Information

  • To send the price-drop alerts for the items and categories you watch
  • To display current public deals from stores in Kuwait
  • To diagnose technical issues and improve app stability
🔗

5. Third-Party Stores

Dealzaty links you to stores such as Xcite, Taw9eel, Blink, Eyewa and others. When you open a deal, you leave Dealzaty and go to that store. We do not process or store anything about your activity on those stores after you leave. Their handling of your data is governed by their own privacy policies.

Final prices are always confirmed on the store you buy from. Dealzaty is an independent service and is not affiliated with any store listed.

🛡️

6. Storage and Security

  • Our servers are hosted on Railway, a cloud infrastructure provider based in the United States
  • All traffic between the app and our servers uses HTTPS/TLS
  • We apply industry-standard measures to protect data in transit and at rest
🗂️

7. Data Retention

  • Your watches are kept until you remove them or delete your data in the app
  • Device-stored data (language, cart) remains until you clear it or uninstall the app
  • Diagnostics are retained only for a limited period for debugging
✅

8. Your Choices and Rights

  • Delete your data at any time from Settings then Delete My Data. This removes your watches and the push token linked to your device
  • Turn notifications on or off from iOS Settings then Notifications then Dealzaty
  • Request access to data associated with your device by emailing support@embur.world
👶

9. Children's Privacy

Dealzaty is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided information, contact support@embur.world and we will delete it.

🌍

10. International Transfers

Our servers are located in the United States. By using Dealzaty from Kuwait, you consent to the transfer of the limited data described above to the United States for processing, subject to the protections in this policy.

🔄

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, a notice in the app. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.

✉️
